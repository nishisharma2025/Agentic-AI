# Business Use Case - PM Workorder Automation

This document describes the business scenario for automated preventive maintenance (PM) with inspections triggering corrective work orders in IBM/Maximo 8.x.

Asset owners want to reduce automated failures, ensure legal traceability, and increase veisibility of asset status and maintenance history. The solution covers: 

- Configuring PM schedules to generate inspection work orders for both time-based and use-based maintenance
- Using Maximo Inspections forms to standardize data capture for structured checks and data driven decisions
- Automatic creation of corrective work orders based on failures or non-conforming findings in the inspection results
- Integration with external ERP/Finance systems and IoT data to improve planning, budgeting, and asset performance monitoring
