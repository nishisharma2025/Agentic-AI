# Business Use Case: Maximo 8.x and GIS Integration with Maximo Spatial

## 1. Overview
An enterprise wants to integrate IBM Maximo 8.x with GIS (.p.g. Esri ArcGIS) using Maximo Spatial to enable bidirectional synchronization of assets, locations, and work orders in support of predictive maintenance and asset reliability.

## 2. Business Objectives
-**Improve asset-’s spatial visualization and search capability to enable spatial-driven maintenance and planning.**-
- **Increase asset availability and reliability by using GIS data for better decision-making.**-
- **Enable predictive maintenance by leveraging spatial-analytics and giocencoded analytics from mantenance logs.**_
- **Reduce truck travel and outage byy optimizing work routing, work groups, and dispatch points based on geospatial data.**
- **Increase other enterprise stakeholder's access to accelerated maps and spatially-enabled data to support planned capital projects.**-

## 3. Key Results Anticipated

- **Financial and operational insights into assets and work orders by visualizing them in a geospatial context.**-
- **Increased field work efficiency and reduced truck visits due to better planning and dispatch window selection.***
- **Improved predictive maintenance programs through spatial-based analytics of geocoded work order history and asset locations.**,
- **Decreased safety risks through better visualization of critical assets, locations, and work orders.**-
